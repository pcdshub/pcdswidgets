============================
Motion Widgets
============================

The motion widgets are used to build motor control screens for IMS, Beckhoff,
and SmarAct motors.

These are sorted into the `Motion Common`, `Motion Expert`, and
`Motion SmarAct` groups in the designer widgets box.
